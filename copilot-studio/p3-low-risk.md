# Phase 3: the easy ones, if the data supports it

Goal: take legal out of the loop for low-risk, repetitive work, with the numbers to justify it. Routine matters go straight back to Sales with the assumptions flagged. The measures from the earlier phases show the time saved and that quality held.

## Risk tiers

| Tier | Meaning | Who answers Sales |
|---|---|---|
| A, routine | An approved low-risk category, and every condition met | The agent, with assumptions flagged |
| B, prepared | Everything else that is not sensitive | The lawyer, from the agent's ticket and draft |
| C, lawyer only | Sensitive matters, disputes, regulators, anything above the value threshold, any condition the agent cannot check | The lawyer; the agent only files |

Every matter starts as B. It becomes A only when it meets every condition of an approved category.

## A low-risk category

Each category is a legal-library entry, approved by the lawyer as a fixed point:

| Field | Example (invented) |
|---|---|
| Name | Mutual NDA on our template, unchanged |
| What is in | Our current mutual NDA template, signed as it stands, for a sales opportunity |
| What is out | Any change to the template; a non-compete; a party in a sanctioned country; a public-sector party |
| Conditions the agent checks | Template version matches; no tracked changes; our signing company is right; the other party passes the sanctions screen |
| The answer | Fixed text with the signing route and who signs |
| Assumptions to flag | For example: "We have assumed this is for the Acme bid only" |
| Evidence | At least 30 matters, at least 90% accepted unchanged, no material error in the last 30 ([measures.md](measures.md)) |
| Approved by, on, review by | The lawyer; dates |

## Answering and closing

- The answer goes to Sales with every assumption flagged and the reference ([templates.md](templates.md)). If Sales correct an assumption, the matter goes back to tier B.
- The matter is marked closed by the agent ([CONTRACT.md](CONTRACT.md), rule 2).
- **Weekly sample:** the lawyer reviews a share of tier A matters (**To confirm:** 10%, at least five).
- **Stop rule:** one material error in a category stops it at once. Its matters go back to tier B until the lawyer has reviewed the error and confirmed the category again.

## Routing

A routing table in the legal library, approved by the lawyer, sends what is not legal's to the right team. Invented examples:

| Topic | Team |
|---|---|
| Discounts, pricing approvals, non-standard payment terms | Deal desk |
| Tax treatment, invoicing | Tax |
| Personal data questions without a contract | Data protection |
| Export licences, sanctions screening | Trade compliance |

The person gets the fixed "routed" text with the team's contact. Routing never changes the register's record that the request came to legal.

## The business case

From [measures.md](measures.md): lawyer hours saved (minutes per matter before, times the tier A volume), time to answer for Sales, the error rate in the samples, and the cost in Copilot Credits and Foundry, set against the Phase 0 baseline.
