# The question bank

"Find out, assume, then ask." The questions legal needs answered before it can start on a type of work: where the answer may already be, what to assume when it is not there, and when an assumption is not safe. It is the most valuable new asset in the plan: it grows out of the Phase 1 question log, the lab turns it into approved entries, and Phase 2 runs on it.

## An entry

One entry per question, in the legal library:

| Field | Meaning | Example (invented) |
|---|---|---|
| ID | QB plus a number | QB-0007 |
| Question | As Sales would read it, one sentence | Which of our companies is signing? |
| Why we ask | What the answer unblocks | We check that company's signing authority and the right template |
| Work types | Where it applies | NDA review; customer contract review |
| Where to find it | Places the answer may already be | The draft's parties clause; the customer's earlier matters; the CRM account owner's region |
| Default | What to assume when it is not found | The company in the customer's country, from the list of our companies |
| Safe to assume when | The conditions | One of our companies operates in that country, and the deal is not cross-border |
| Never assume when | When the agent must ask | Public tenders; the customer asks for a different company; the value is above the threshold |
| Who answers | The role | The account owner |
| Origin | Where it came from | Question log, 9 matters; lawyer's interview, 2026-11-20 |
| Approved by, on, review by | Approval | The lawyer; dates |

The question, why we ask and the stated assumption may be shown to Sales. Everything else stays in the legal library.

## How it grows

1. **Phase 1:** every question legal asks is logged ([p1-agent-and-button.md](p1-agent-and-button.md)).
2. **Monthly:** the paralegal groups questions that ask for the same thing and marks the ones asked three or more times as candidates.
3. **In the lab, by watching:** the lab records every question legal puts to a requester before it can start, from legal's own emails, with the answer when it comes. Before legal answers a new request, the lab notes which questions it expects; afterwards it compares, and a question it missed is added. Each legal skill's "Questions we ask first" section is built from these: the question, why we ask, where the answer may already be, what legal went ahead on when it was missing. A guided learning session or an interview adds the lawyer's own words where the lawyer wants to. The lab drafts the entries from that section.
4. **The lawyer approves** the entries in one sitting before export. A default and its "never assume when" are fixed points: the lab may show what legal went ahead on in past matters, but only the lawyer's own word makes it a default.
5. **Export** to the legal library ([export.md](export.md)); the question and its reason also go into the Sales "what legal needs from you" guide.

## How it is checked

- **Phase 1.5:** drafted questions should come from approved entries. A question the lawyer adds ("missed") that recurs becomes a candidate.
- **Phase 2:** count assumptions that Sales corrected, per entry. An entry corrected often has its default withdrawn until the lawyer reviews it ([measures.md](measures.md)).
