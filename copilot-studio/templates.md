# Fixed texts

Plain, short, the same every time. `<...>` is filled in by the flow; nothing else changes without the lawyer's yes. Every subject ends with `[<reference>]`.

## Receipt (Phase 1, from the button)

> Subject: `<original subject> [<reference>]`
>
> Thanks, legal has your request, reference `<reference>`. Someone will pick it up and contact you. If it is urgent, reply to this email and say why. Please keep `[<reference>]` in the subject of any email about it.

## Acknowledgement (Phase 1.5 on, from `legal-intake@`)

> Subject: `<original subject> [<reference>]`
>
> Thanks, legal has received your email, reference `<reference>`. We will come back to you, with any questions first. Please keep `[<reference>]` in the subject of any email about it.

## Follow-up questions (Phase 1.5 after approval; Phase 2 directly)

> Subject: `<original subject> [<reference>]`
>
> To get started on `<reference>` we need a few answers:
> 1. `<question>` (`<why, one line>`)
> 2. ...
>
> We have assumed the following. Tell us if any of it is wrong:
> - `<assumption>`
>
> Reply to this email with your answers. If we hear nothing by `<date>`, we will go ahead on these assumptions.

The assumptions block appears in Phase 2 only. Default limit: four questions per email (**To confirm**).

## Reminder (Phase 2)

> Subject: `<original subject> [<reference>]`
>
> A reminder: we still need your answers on `<reference>` before we can start. The questions are in our email of `<date>`.

## Routed elsewhere

> Subject: `<original subject> [<reference>]`
>
> This one is for `<team>`, not legal: `<reason, one line>`. Please contact them at `<contact>`. We have kept a note of it under `<reference>`.

## Answer with assumptions (Phase 3, approved categories only)

> Subject: `<original subject> [<reference>]`
>
> `<the category's fixed answer>`
>
> This answer is based on these assumptions. If any is wrong, reply before you go ahead, and legal will look at it:
> - `<assumption>`

## Claim card

Reference; subject; requester and team; other party; what they need; due date; attachments (number); five-point flags; questions waiting (Phase 1.5 on). Actions: Claim; Not for legal; Open folder; Log a question.

## Ticket for the lawyer (Phase 2)

The lab's brief layout, one page:
- **Requester**
- **What they want**
- **By when**
- **What we still need from them** (none, once the file is complete)
- **What the lawyer must decide**
- **Proposed outcome**
- **Proposed next step**

Then links: the thread, the key documents, the answers and assumptions, the first draft.
