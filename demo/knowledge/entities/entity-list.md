# Kestrel group entity list

## Summary

The Kestrel group has two companies. Kestrel Engineering Ltd in Sheffield is the parent: it designs and makes the pumps, sells them worldwide and runs the remote condition monitoring service. Kestrel Pumpen GmbH in Dortmund is its wholly owned German subsidiary: sales, service and final assembly for Germany, Austria and Switzerland. Contracts must name the right company, and each company signs in its own way (see the delegation of authority policy).

## Points that matter in contracts

- The representation rule for Kestrel Pumpen GmbH is entered in the Handelsregister: "Die Gesellschaft wird durch zwei Geschäftsführer gemeinsam vertreten." One Geschäftsführer alone cannot bind the company, and an officer of Kestrel Engineering Ltd who is not a Geschäftsführer (for example the group CFO) cannot sign for the GmbH without a written power of attorney (Vollmacht) from both Geschäftsführer (Handelsregisterauszug of 2026-09-28; delegation of authority policy s 6.3).
- Customer contracts for the UK and the rest of the world are made by Kestrel Engineering Ltd. Customer contracts for Germany, Austria and Switzerland are usually made by Kestrel Pumpen GmbH, unless the customer buys centrally from the UK company (delegation of authority policy s 6.1).
- The remote condition monitoring service is always provided by Kestrel Engineering Ltd, which runs the platform, even where the pumps are sold by the GmbH (entity register note of 2026-09-30).
- Group insurance covers both companies (insurance schedule 2026/27, knowledge/entities/insurance-schedule-2026.pdf).
