# Liability caps in customer contracts

## Summary

How much liability Kestrel accepts in customer contracts for pumps, spare parts, service and remote condition monitoring. The standard cap is 100% of the annual fees. Data breach may get a separate, higher cap of up to 2x the annual fees with Legal's approval. Kestrel never accepts unlimited liability, except for death or personal injury and fraud. One exception: customers regulated by the PRA or FCA may get up to 3x for data breach, with the CFO's approval, because their regulators' outsourcing rules require it. Anything above 1x for the general cap needs the CFO.

## Key facts

- Standard general cap: 100% of the annual fees, per contract year (LP-03 s 2.1).
- Data breach: a separate super-cap of up to 2x the annual fees, with Legal approval (LP-03 s 3.1; delegation of authority policy s 5).
- Never uncapped, except for death or personal injury caused by negligence and for fraud, and any other liability that the law does not allow us to limit (LP-03 s 4.1).
- Exception for PRA- or FCA-regulated financial services customers: up to 3x the annual fees for data breach, with CFO approval, because their regulators' outsourcing rules require adequate liability terms from material suppliers (LP-03 s 5.1). Precedent: a 2025 bank deal for the maintenance of data-centre cooling pumps (LP-03 s 5.3).
- A general cap above 1x the annual fees needs CFO approval (delegation of authority policy s 5).
- Indirect and consequential loss, including loss of profit and loss of production, is excluded for both parties (LP-03 s 6.1).

## Positions

| Point | Preferred | Fallback, and who approves | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| General cap (LP-03 s 2) | 100% of the annual fees per contract year | Above 100%, up to 200%: CFO, after Legal review | Above 200%; a cap set at the whole contract value of a multi-year deal |
| Data breach (LP-03 s 3) | Inside the general cap | Separate super-cap up to 2x the annual fees: Legal | Unlimited |
| Regulated financial services (LP-03 s 5) | Separate super-cap up to 2x the annual fees | Up to 3x the annual fees, only where the PRA or FCA outsourcing rules apply to the contract: CFO | Unlimited; uncapped regulatory fines |
| Regulatory fines (LP-03 s 5.2) | Excluded, or inside the data breach super-cap | Inside the 3x super-cap for a regulated customer: CFO | Uncapped |
| Unlimited heads (LP-03 s 4) | Death or personal injury caused by negligence; fraud; liability that cannot be limited by law | None | Unlimited data breach; uncapped indemnities |
| Indirect loss (LP-03 s 6) | Mutual exclusion, including loss of profit and loss of production | Costs of production stoppage caused by defective products recoverable as direct loss, but only inside the cap: Legal | Liability for indirect or consequential loss outside the cap |

"Annual fees" means the fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the event that gives rise to the claim (LP-03 s 1.2). Check the definition in every draft: some customers use the whole contract value instead.

## Why

- General cap of 100%: the margin on equipment and service does not support more, and higher caps raise our insurance cost (LP-03 s 2.2).
- Data breach: the monitoring service connects to customers' networks, so customers ask for more than the general cap. Our cyber insurance is GBP 5 million for all claims in the policy year, shared across every customer. A super-cap of 2x stays insurable at our usual contract sizes; unlimited liability is not insurable (LP-03 s 3.2; insurance schedule 2026/27).
- Regulated financial services: banks and other regulated firms must manage outsourcing and third-party risk to their regulators' expectations (PRA Supervisory Statement SS2/21; FCA rules in SYSC 8). Their supplier policies require higher caps for data breach from suppliers that support important business services, and they cannot sign below them. The exception applies only where the contract is an outsourcing or material third-party arrangement for that customer, not to a one-off equipment sale (LP-03 s 5.1).
- Never unlimited: unlimited liability, an uncapped indemnity or uncapped regulatory fines could exceed the value of the contract many times over, and liability we take on by contract beyond what the law imposes is excluded from our liability insurance (LP-03 s 4.2).

## Contracts under German law

Customers in Germany often use their own terms under German law. Under German law, liability for intent and gross negligence, and for injury to life, body or health, cannot be excluded or limited in standard business terms (s 309 no 7 BGB, applied between businesses through s 307 BGB), and liability under the Product Liability Act (Produkthaftungsgesetz) cannot be limited at all. Expect these carve-outs in German customer paper: they are mandatory, so accepting them is not a departure from this position. A cap for ordinary negligence must still cover the damage typical and foreseeable for the contract. A clause that was individually negotiated is not a standard term (s 305(1) BGB), so keep the record of how the liability clause was negotiated (LP-03 s 7).

## How to apply

1. Find the annual fees and the expected value of the contract from the requester.
2. Compare the proposed caps with this page and say which line each point falls on.
3. Check that the exposure fits the insurance in knowledge/entities/insurance-schedule-2026.pdf.
4. Name the approver for every point above "Preferred", from the delegation of authority policy.
5. Present options with a view. The lawyer decides; the approver approves before anything is signed.

## Where used

- 2025: maintenance agreement for the cooling-water pumps at the data centres of a PRA-regulated UK bank. General cap 100%; data breach super-cap 3x, approved by the CFO; regulatory fines inside the 3x cap, not uncapped (contract register CR-2025-031; LP-03 s 5.3).
- 2025: supply and monitoring agreement with a building society. Data breach super-cap 2x, approved by Legal; uncapped regulatory fines refused. The society's outsourcing team confirmed the contract was not a material outsourcing, so the 3x exception did not apply (contract register CR-2025-044).
