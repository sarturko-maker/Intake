# NDA positions

## Summary

What Kestrel accepts in NDAs and confidentiality agreements with customers, suppliers, partners and bidders. We prefer a mutual NDA on our own template, for up to three years (five for trade secrets), under English law. Some things never go into an NDA: a non-compete, a non-solicitation of customers, an indemnity, or confidentiality that never ends except for trade secrets. Legal reviews every NDA that is not on our template unchanged; signing follows the delegation of authority policy.

## Key facts

- Mutual NDA preferred (LP-01 s 1).
- Term up to 3 years from signature; 5 years for trade secrets (LP-01 s 2).
- Governing law: England and Wales, with the English courts (LP-01 s 3).
- Never a non-compete or a non-solicitation of customers in an NDA (LP-01 s 4).
- No indemnity in an NDA; never an uncapped indemnity (LP-01 s 5).
- No perpetual confidentiality, except for trade secrets while they remain trade secrets (LP-01 s 2.3).
- Our template: knowledge/templates/kestrel-mutual-nda-template.docx (LP-01 s 8).
- Signing: any director of Kestrel Engineering Ltd or the Senior Legal Counsel; for Kestrel Pumpen GmbH, its two Geschäftsführer jointly (delegation of authority policy s 6.2).

## Positions

| Point | Preferred | Fallback, and who agrees | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper (LP-01 s 8) | Kestrel mutual NDA template | The other side's paper, if mutual and close to these positions: Legal | |
| Form (LP-01 s 1) | Mutual | One-way in the other side's favour, only where Kestrel will disclose nothing confidential (for example a site visit): Legal | One-way in their favour where we will share prices, drawings, designs or monitoring data |
| Term (LP-01 s 2) | 3 years from signature; 5 years for trade secrets | Up to 5 years for all information; trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets: Legal | Perpetual confidentiality for all information, or no end date |
| Law and courts (LP-01 s 3) | England and Wales | The other side's home law in the UK, the EU or the EEA, if the NDA is otherwise on these positions: Legal. NDAs of Kestrel Pumpen GmbH: German law is the standard | Anything else without Legal |
| Non-compete and exclusivity (LP-01 s 4.1) | None | None | Any restriction on whom we may sell to, bid for or work with |
| Non-solicitation of customers (LP-01 s 4.2) | None | None | Any |
| Non-solicitation of employees (LP-01 s 4.3) | None | Mutual, at most 12 months, only staff directly involved, with a carve-out for general recruitment advertising: Legal | One-way, or longer than 12 months |
| Indemnity (LP-01 s 5) | None: damages and an injunction are the remedies | None | Any indemnity, and above all an uncapped one |
| Penalties (LP-01 s 5.2) | None | None | Liquidated damages or penalty clauses for breach |
| Residuals (LP-01 s 6) | None | None | A residuals clause in the other side's favour |
| Security obligations (LP-01 s 7) | Reasonable care, at least the care we take with our own information | A security schedule fixed at signature and agreed by IT Security | Security rules the other side can change on notice |
| Return and destruction (LP-01 s 9) | On request, keeping copies required by law and routine back-ups | Written confirmation of destruction within a reasonable time after a request: fine | A duty to destroy routine back-ups |
| Assignment (LP-01 s 10) | Neither party without consent; transfers within the group allowed | One-sided group transfer right for the other side: Legal | |

## Why the never lines are never

- An NDA is for sharing information, not for limiting where we can sell. A non-compete or a non-solicitation of customers would restrict our sales in a market for years, for the price of seeing some documents (LP-01 s 4.4).
- An indemnity turns a confidentiality slip into open-ended liability for the other side's costs. It is not insurable and our liability insurance does not cover liability we take on by contract (LP-01 s 5.3).
- Perpetual confidentiality creates obligations nobody can track. Only trade secrets need protection for as long as they stay secret (LP-01 s 2.3).

## Process

1. Requester sends the NDA to legal@kestrel-eng.co.uk with the purpose, what we will disclose and receive, and any deadline (LP-01 s 11).
2. Legal answers on the same or the next working day.
3. Mark-ups go back as tracked changes with a short comment on each change.
4. Kestrel signs only after Legal has cleared the text; the signatory follows the delegation of authority policy.
5. Signed NDAs go to Legal for the contract register.
